# Privacy Policy for Veil-Break

**Last updated: June 8, 2026**

## Overview

Veil-Break ("the app") is developed by Michael Carrihill. This policy explains our data practices.

## Data Collection

Veil-Break does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app:

- Does not require account creation or login
- Does not collect names, email addresses, or contact information
- Does not collect location data
- Does not track usage analytics or behaviour
- Does not share any data with third parties

## Advertising

The app may display advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google's privacy policy governs data collected by AdMob. You can review it at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Game Progress

Game progress (levels completed, stars earned) is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to any server.

## Children

The app is rated 4+ and is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

This policy may be updated occasionally. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
